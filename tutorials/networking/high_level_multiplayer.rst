Native networking in EGP
========================

EGP integrates native Superpos. Godot scene RPC/peer APIs and the earlier
transport helpers are retired. See :doc:`/egp/networking_reference` and
:doc:`/egp/superpos_migration`. Optional RTC core work does not establish browser
engine exports or an enabled engine RTC provider.
