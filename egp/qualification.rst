Support and qualification
=========================

EGP is a Godot 4.8 development fork. Its active desktop networking integration
is Superpos. The previous transport, Superposition nodes, language facades and
old arena/network-lab receipts are retired.

The adapter ledger records isolated native API, unsigned metadata, owner
lifecycle, UDP, managed and C++17 checks. These do not qualify the current full
engine graph, templates, rendering, solver recovery or WAN gameplay. The fixed
engine receipt ``.build/diagnostics/superpos-cutover.json`` records current
build/runtime results. Passing source checks alone is not runtime evidence.

Rendered projects use Forward+ through Vulkan, Direct3D 12 or Metal. Web exports
are unsupported. visionOS immersive Forward+ is an opt-in experimental path;
physical headset rendering/tracking/performance/lifecycle remain unqualified.
See :doc:`visionos_experimental`, :doc:`xmake`, :doc:`networking_reference`
and :doc:`superpos_migration`.
