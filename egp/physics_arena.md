# Legacy physics arena

This route belongs to the retired networking integration. Its old APIs and
fixtures are not supported by the active engine. See [Superpos](networking_reference.md)
and [migration](superpos_migration.md) for the replacement and qualification limits.

Current examples are the physics showcase and remote courier arena described
in [the network lab guide](network_lab.md). Their canonical projection and
local solver snapshots do not restore the former networking adapter contract.
